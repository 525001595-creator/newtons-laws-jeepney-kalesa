1st Law — Inertia

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion unless acted on by a net force.

🧍

Passenger Inertia

When the jeepney changes motion suddenly, the passenger tends to keep the previous state of motion.

2

2nd Law — F = ma

The acceleration of an object depends on the net force acting on it and its mass.

3

3rd Law — Action & Reaction

For every action force, there is an equal in magnitude and opposite reaction force.
